# Consent sheet: AeroFleet VR debrief study

**COEP Technological University, final-year project AeroFleet 2.0.** Guide: Dr. N. K. Chougule.

**What we are testing.** Whether looking at drone flights in a VR headset makes it easier to see if a flight left its
planned route than looking at a map on a laptop. We are testing the software, not you.

**What you will do (about 25 minutes, seated).**
1. On a laptop, look at 18 drone flights (3 practice) and say for each whether it stayed on its plan, went off
   sideways or went off in height.
2. Wearing a Meta Quest 3 headset, do the same for 18 different flights, choosing with the controller.
   (Some participants do the headset part first.)
3. Say which way was easier.

**Risks.** Some people feel dizzy or uneasy in VR. You stay seated, the headset part takes about 10 minutes, and you
can take it off at any moment.

**Your data.** You get a code (for example P07). We store only that code, your answers, how long each took and your
preference. No name, photo, video or audio is recorded. This sheet, which has your name, is kept by the guide and is
not linked to the stored answers. Results are reported only as totals.

**Your choice.** Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time without giving a reason; whatever you have answered
is then deleted if you ask.
