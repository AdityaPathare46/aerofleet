AeroFleet · flight records for ArduPilot & PX4 fleets

Every flight checked against its approved plan, and the record kept so nobody can quietly change it.

Upload the flight log and the mission file. AeroFleet tells you whether the drone flew what was approved, which settings were different, and what happened when. Every result goes into a signed record you can hand to an auditor, an insurer or an accident investigator.

The problem

  • Operators flying under a UK or EU authorisation must keep a technical log for each aircraft: checks, defects and configuration changes, kept for at least 3 years and protected from alteration.
  • Logbook tools are built around DJI. DJI stopped flight-log sync for US users in June 2024, and the tools we checked do not compare an ArduPilot or PX4 flight with its mission or track setting changes.
  • After an incident, someone opens dozens of plots by hand. The PX4 forum alone has about 410 crash threads where people post a log and ask what happened.

Who it is for

Survey, inspection, agriculture and delivery operators running five or more ArduPilot or PX4 drones under an operational authorisation, who need flight records that stand up in an audit or after an incident. Also usable for the 48-hour accident reports required in India and the 10-day reports in the US.
