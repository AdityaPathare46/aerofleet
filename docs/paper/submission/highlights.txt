Highlights
- No rule or model separated pilot-caused from aircraft-caused crashes
- Grouping folds by vehicle removed part of an apparent crash-detection signal
- The usual tilt rule misses 7-50% of multicopter crashes, by rater
- Crashed drones vibrated more than peers, but did not beat random inspection
- A re-implemented fault detector fell from AUC 0.94 to 0.63 with days held out
