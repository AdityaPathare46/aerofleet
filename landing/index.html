The council proposes.

AeroFleet plans city-scale drone deliveries with a 16-agent LLM council — dispatch, routing, battery, weather, compliance, and more, each grounded in its own domain. But no council output ever moves a drone. A deterministic Control-Barrier-Function gate, evaluated in under a millisecond, is the only thing that can approve or reject a dispatch — and it can't be talked into an exception.

Three deterministic checks before anything flies

01

16-agent council

Dispatch, route, battery, weather, comms, cost, compliance, cybersecurity, and more — each a specialist LLM agent reasoning over its own domain, running open-weight, non-Chinese-origin models (Llama, Mistral, Gemma, Phi) end to end.

02

CBF safety gate

Every candidate dispatch is checked against 11 named Control-Barrier-Function constraints — separation, geofence, battery reserve, altitude ceiling, wind, payload, noise, collision probability, link margin, depot capacity, visibility — in well under a millisecond. No LLM sits on this path.

03

Post-hoc, not pre-hoc

The council's reasoning is never on the critical path of a decision. When the gate rejects a dispatch, a separate Fleet Incident Forensics Council investigates why, asynchronously — producing a root-cause report a human reviews before any policy threshold changes.

Verify the AI, don't just trust it

AeroFleet's VR Safety View has two modes. Live mode renders the CBF gate's real-time safety margins in stereoscopic 3D. Incident Replay reconstructs a rejected dispatch's frozen geometry — real coordinates, real violated constraints — so an operator can check the Forensics Council's narrative against what actually happened, in the headset, rather than trusting a paragraph of AI-generated text on faith.

Real hardware, not just simulation

A MAVLink hardware link talks to live drones directly — the same CBF certificate that gates a simulated dispatch gates a real one. Simulated and live drones run side by side in the same fleet view.

Human-gated policy, always

The council can recommend a threshold change after an incident. It can never apply one. Every recommended policy change becomes a pending proposal an operator must explicitly approve before it takes effect.

Try it yourself

Clone the repo, or grab the installer for your platform. First launch walks you through installing the local AI models with a guided setup wizard — no manual commands to copy.
